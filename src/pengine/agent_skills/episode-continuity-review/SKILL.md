# Episode continuity review

Treat the story contract and prior series state as immutable. Review only the
unlocked candidate episode and its proposed state delta.

Fail on identity drift, changed relationships, uncontracted dates or measured
numbers, viewpoint knowledge withheld unfairly, missing visible or audible clue
evidence, an unresolved required clue, a missing new-information beat, or a weak
end hook. Cite the exact contract identifiers and script excerpts.

Do not repair content and do not reinterpret the contract to make the draft pass.
