# Episode continuity review

Treat the story contract, approved upstream artifacts, prior series state, and
committed earlier episodes as immutable. Review the unlocked candidate episode,
its proposed state delta, and the complete committed series prefix. On the last
episode, this prefix is the whole series and must pass before the script stage
can be approved.

Fail on identity or alias drift, changed relationships, wrong pronouns,
uncontracted dates or measured numbers, changed ages or elapsed durations,
changed call participants, clue-meaning substitution, causal contradiction,
viewpoint knowledge withheld unfairly, missing visible or audible clue evidence,
an unresolved required clue, a missing new-information beat, or a weak end hook.
Compare the candidate to every prior script, not only the latest state summary.
Cite the exact contract identifiers and script excerpts.

Do not repair content and do not reinterpret the contract to make the draft pass.
