# Episode continuity review

Treat the story contract, approved upstream artifacts, prior series state, and
committed earlier episodes as immutable. Review the unlocked candidate episode,
its proposed state delta, and the complete committed series prefix. On the last
episode, this prefix is the whole series and must pass before the script stage
can be approved.

For this review, hard Canon is limited to explicit locked contract values,
formally committed facts and state in the prefix, mandatory episode
obligations, and the output/schema protocol. Ordinary prose in an approved
artifact is context, not an automatic lock. Fail only when the candidate
contradicts that hard Canon: identity or alias drift,
changed relationships, wrong pronouns, changed dates or measured numbers,
changed ages or elapsed durations, changed call participants, clue-meaning
substitution, causal contradiction, viewpoint knowledge withheld unfairly,
missing visible or audible evidence for a required clue, an unresolved required
clue, a missing required new-information beat, or a weak locked end hook. A
detail that the contract and committed prefix leave unspecified is free for the
writer to choose and is not a review failure.
Compare the candidate to every prior script, not only the latest state summary.
Cite the exact contract identifiers and script excerpts.

Do not repair content and do not reinterpret the contract to make the draft pass.
